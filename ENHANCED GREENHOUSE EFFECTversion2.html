Learning Objectives

1. Define greenhouse gases and the enhanced greenhouse effect.
2. Identify major greenhouse gases and their anthropogenic sources.
3. Explain how excess greenhouse gases drive global warming and climate change.
4. Complete a cause-and-effect flowchart connecting human action to global shifts.
5. Propose practical mitigation steps for home, school, and local community.

Key takeaway

Natural GHGs keep Earth at a habitable average of ~15°C. Human activities pump excess GHGs, creating an Enhanced Greenhouse Effect that traps disproportionate heat.

Discussion & Processing Questions

  • Why are greenhouse gases naturally important for sustaining life on Earth?
  • What happens to atmospheric temperature when human activities drastically increase GHG levels?
  • How is the enhanced greenhouse effect directly related to global warming and climate change?
